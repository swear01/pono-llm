## Interpretation

Transition context produced **better structured** candidates (tsyn_002 and
tsyn_003 use OR consequents — a novel pattern not seen before). However,
the transition slices for the key variables (state1536, state790) are too
deeply nested for the LLM to extract causal structure from.

The readable variables (state1558, state79, partially state2002) have simple
transition logic but don't pair with state1536 in a way that yields inductive
lemmas.

\*One was solver-verified but downgraded to trivial

### Key Insight

Three experiments (reachability-aware, transition-aware, resynthesis)
all produce candidates that pass ALL pre-solver gates (reachable filter,
nontriviality, init) but fail one-step induction. This suggests:

1. The pre-gates are effective at filtering doomed candidates
2. The remaining failures are genuinely hard — the transition system has
   reachable states that violate the proposed relations
3. The LLM lacks access to the FULL transition relation needed to verify
   inductiveness before proposing

## Parser Extension

Added `(=> (= X V) (or (= Y W) (= Z U)))` pattern to `lemma_to_smt` to
support OR consequents generated by transition-aware synthesis.

## Recommended Next Step

The bottleneck is now clear: the LLM cannot evaluate inductiveness without
running the transition relation. Two possible directions:

1. **Integrate solver feedback into the LLM loop**: after each candidate,
   run one-step check and feed back exact counterexample values (closing
   the synthesis loop analogous to the qspiflash repair loop but for
   synthesis rather than repair).

2. **Provide better transition summaries**: improve the BTOR2 explainer to
   produce deeper, more readable pseudo-code for complex ITE chains,
   especially for state1536 which is central to most lemmas.
